# Appliances – Teinar

URL: https://teinar.is/en/appliances/

Appliances – Teinar
Appliances
At the end of treatment appliances are fitted in both gums.
Appliances in the upper gum, glued to the inside of the teeth, glued to each tooth from eyetooth to eyetooth.
We recommend that the appliances are kept in place for about 15 years.
A support arch in the lower gum, glued to the eye teeth.
We recommend that the support arch in the lower gum be never removed.
