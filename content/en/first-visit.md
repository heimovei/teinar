First visit – Teinar
First visit
The first visit involves tooth inspection and a chat, sometimes an oral image taken for further diagnosis, particularly where teeth have not emerged. This visit will take from 30 minutes up to one hour. We request that parents accompany children and teenagers and where this is not possible, then a telephone call at the end of the visit is the next best option.
